<h1 class="labs-page-title"><span class="labs-page-heading__name">3DBAG</span><span class="labs-page-heading__labs">Labs</span></h1>

The <span class="labs-page-title__inline"><span class="labs-page-title__name">3DBAG</span><span class="labs-page-title__labs">Labs</span></span> is a collection of experiments demos for the [3DBAG project](https://3dbag.nl).
They showcase proof of concepts that are not yet ready for production or may never be ready for production, but are interesting to explore.
The experiments are maintained independently of the 3DBAG project by their corresponding authors.
